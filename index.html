About the Campus

ISUFST – San Enrique Campus

The Iloilo State University of Fisheries Science and Technology (ISUFST) – San Enrique Campus is one of the campuses of the university committed to providing quality education, developing competent students, and promoting innovation, leadership, and community engagement.

The campus provides an environment where students can develop their academic knowledge, technical skills, leadership abilities, and values through various educational programs and campus activities.

Through its faculty, staff, administrators, and student organizations, the San Enrique Campus continues to support students in achieving academic excellence while preparing them to become responsible and productive members of society.
